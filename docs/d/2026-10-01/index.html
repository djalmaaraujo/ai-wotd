Gemini 4 Argon

Gemini 4 Argon trended because Google officially announced the model today via its blog and the Latent Space newsletter, revealing benchmark-leading performance, a 1M-token output capability, and a gated rollout to cybersecurity defenders through the Fairwind Program — all framed as Google DeepMind's answer to competing frontier models from rivals.

Definition

Gemini 4 Argon is Google DeepMind's newest flagship large language model, designed for deep, long-horizon reasoning across software engineering, enterprise knowledge work, and cybersecurity defense. It features an experimental Long Decode Continuation that supports up to 1 million output tokens, far exceeding typical context windows. The model is initially available only to vetted security researchers via Google's Fairwind Program while the company completes voluntary U.S. government pre-release safety reviews.

Today in AI

Google unveiled Gemini 4 Argon, its latest frontier model, positioning it as a direct response to rival systems like Astra and Fable. The model achieves state-of-the-art results on 13 of 19 credible benchmarks and introduces an experimental Long Decode Continuation that pushes output tokens to 1 million — an industry first. Access is initially restricted to a cybersecurity preview via Google's Fairwind Program, targeting trusted defenders for tasks such as vulnerability hunting and patching. The company says it is simultaneously engaging with the U.S. government's voluntary pre-release access process before broadening availability. The launch follows a period of incremental Flash releases and a management shakeup at Google DeepMind, marking the lab's first larger-than-Flash model since Gemini 3.1 Pro in February.
