Holo4

Holo4 trended today because H Company published a launch blog on Hugging Face announcing the model family's immediate availability on their API, releasing two model sizes plus Holotron4 Nano with open weights in multiple quantization formats and accompanying trajectory datasets. The announcement emphasizes production-ready computer-use agents rather than academic benchmarks, drawing attention from practitioners building automation workflows.

Definition

Holo4 is a family of agentic foundation models developed by H Company for generalist computer-use tasks. The models interact with software environments through graphical interfaces, code execution, Model Context Protocol (MCP), and APIs, enabling autonomous execution of multi-step workflows. Released in 27B dense and 35B-A3B MoE configurations, plus a smaller Holotron4 Nano variant, they are trained via supervised and reinforcement learning on synthetic tasks from an Agentic Task Factory. Holo4 represents the emerging category of GUI-capable agents that operate existing software without custom integrations.

Today in AI

H Company launched Holo4, a new series of agentic models designed for generalist computer-use tasks, marking a push toward autonomous software interaction. The release includes a 27B dense model and a 35B-A3B Mixture of Experts variant, both accessible via the H Models API, alongside an updated Holotron4 Nano. Built on supervised and reinforcement learning across diverse environments generated by the company's Agentic Task Factory, Holo4 operates through GUIs, code, MCP, and APIs. The models are distributed in FP16, FP8, and GGUF formats with trajectory datasets for transparency. This release reflects the industry's shift from benchmark-chasing to deploying agents in real business workflows, with H Company positioning itself alongside other labs productizing computer-use capabilities.
