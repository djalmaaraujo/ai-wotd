Depth Pro 2

Depth Pro 2 trended today because Apple published the model weights and documentation on Hugging Face, making three scaled variants immediately available for download and integration. The release emphasizes full-resolution metric depth from ordinary cameras with sharp object boundaries and frame-to-frame consistency, directly addressing needs in AR/VR, robotics, and autonomous systems.

Definition

Depth Pro 2 is Apple's second-generation metric monocular depth estimation model family. It predicts absolute depth (in metric units) for every pixel from a single RGB image or video frame at the input's native resolution, without requiring stereo pairs or known camera intrinsics. The architecture prioritizes sharp depth discontinuities at object edges and temporal consistency across video frames, enabling applications in augmented reality, 3D reconstruction, and robotics. Three model sizes (S, B, L) offer a compute-quality tradeoff, with the largest (334M parameters) delivering the highest edge fidelity and stability.
