Axion 1.0

Axion 1.0 trended today because ByteDance publicly released the complete open-source package — model weights for both 122B and 35B variants, training datasets, reproduction code, and a detailed arXiv paper — all on the same day. The release's emphasis on a minimal three-stage recipe (2 SFT + RL) that matches or exceeds proprietary agents on benchmarks like HLE, FrontierScience, and SEAL-0 sparked immediate discussion across AI research channels.

Definition

Axion 1.0 is a family of open-source agent foundation models developed by ByteDance that combines strong scientific reasoning, tool use, and information-seeking abilities with a streamlined training approach: two lightweight supervised fine-tuning stages followed by reinforcement learning, without mid-training, DPO, or on-policy distillation. Released in 122B dense and 35B Mixture-of-Experts variants, the models are designed for agentic tasks such as multi-step problem solving, web-based research, and code execution. The fully open release includes weights, training data, code, and a reproducible recipe, positioning Axion 1.0 as a community resource for advancing open agent research.

Today in AI

ByteDance released Axion 1.0, a fully open agent foundation model family that achieves frontier-level performance with a radically simplified training recipe — two supervised fine-tuning stages followed by reinforcement learning, omitting mid-training, DPO, and on-policy distillation. The 122B and 35B (MoE) checkpoints are available on Hugging Face alongside training data and code on GitHub. Axion 1.0-122B scores 59.0 on HLE with restricted web access, 87.0 on FrontierScience Olympiad, and 66.7 on FrontierScience Research, while the 35B variant reaches 62.2 on SEAL-0. The project, led by ByteDance researchers with academic collaborators from Duke, UMD, and UB, demonstrates that high-quality data and a lean three-stage pipeline can yield strong scientific reasoning, tool use, and information-seeking capabilities. Meanwhile, other Chinese labs are also making waves: MiniMax and Moonshot appear in today's trending terms, and Xiaomi's AI activity registers heavily in the corpus.
