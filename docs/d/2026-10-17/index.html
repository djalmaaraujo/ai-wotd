nvidia-nemotron-3.5

The term trended due to the release of several new model variants on Hugging Face, specifically the Super-VL-120B for high-precision tasks and the Bolt-74B for local agentic workflows.

Definition

NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 is a family of vision-language models (VLMs) developed by NVIDIA that utilize hybrid architectures, combining Mamba-2, Mixture-of-Experts (MoE), and Transformer layers. These models are designed for multimodal tasks including document understanding, reasoning, and agentic workflows, with specific versions optimized for either maximum intelligence (Super-VL) or local deployment efficiency (Bolt). They represent NVIDIA's effort to provide high-performance, open-weights alternatives for developers needing scalable AI that can handle both text and visual inputs.

  • https://huggingface.co/nvidia

Today in AI

NVIDIA is expanding its open-weights portfolio with the release of the Nemotron 3.5 series, focusing on high-efficiency vision-language models (VLMs). The company has introduced the Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL-120B, a large-scale model designed for complex reasoning, coding, and video understanding with a 1-million token context window. Simultaneously, NVIDIA released the Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-74B, which utilizes a Mamba-2-Transformer hybrid architecture and 2-bit quantization to enable local execution of agentic tasks within 24GB of memory. To further enhance performance, NVIDIA provided a LiLiCorr speculative-decoding checkpoint for the Bolt model to accelerate token generation. These releases highlight a strategic push toward hybrid architectures and memory-optimized models that balance intelligence with deployment speed for both cloud and local environments.
